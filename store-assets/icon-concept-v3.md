# Icon concept v3 — Text to input

Built-in ImageGen generation followed by one refinement. Not applied to the app or Play Console.
Source: `ko-KR/icon-concept-v3-source.png`.
512px RGB export: `ko-KR/icon-concept-v3-512.png` (mechanical System.Drawing downscale).
Concept: text strokes flow into a rightward arrow toward an insertion caret. Deep teal, warm white, mint.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE exceptionally designed app icon for 바로입력, a Korean Android utility which takes printed text and inserts it into the user's chosen text field. This is a serious bespoke brand mark, not a generic camera/OCR icon and not a collection of pictograms.
Concept: "text becomes input". Design a bold single ligature in which two short horizontal text strokes on the left flow into a substantial rightward arrow on the right, ending just before a separate upright insertion caret. The arrow and source text strokes must feel like a single carefully constructed monogram, with clever negative space, NOT a standard login arrow. Prefer a distinctive compact silhouette akin to a custom typographic glyph: two softly squared horizontal white ribbons, the lower ribbon sweeping upward into the broad rightward arrowhead, upper ribbon shorter, all optically integrated. A bright pale mint insertion caret to the right completes the symbol. Keep it extremely clear and balanced, not a diagram. Text strokes should imply text without drawing a literal page.
Visual: deep rich evergreen teal background (#075E57) full bleed with perfectly uniform flat color. Warm white main symbol (#FFFDF5), light fresh mint caret (#9FF1CD). High contrast, mature friendly Korean productivity brand. Crisp vector-like edges, purposeful softly rounded corners, generous breathing space. Very few shapes; bold enough to be recognizable at 32 pixels. Center entire mark within the central 62% square safe area for Android circular and squircle masks. Refined optical spacing.
Output single square finished opaque icon artwork, no border, no rounded outer tile shape, no mockup, no caption.
Avoid all letter characters, Hangul characters, camera lenses, scan brackets, QR codes, keyboard grids, documents, checkmarks, sparkles, circles surrounding the mark, decorative speed lines, 3D, bevels, shadows, glow, texture, gradients, stock clipart, login/door symbols. No words or slogans. Flat brand artwork only.

## Refinement prompt

Refine the supplied app icon concept into a clean finished flat icon. Preserve the concept and overall positions of the two white text strokes flowing into one rightward arrow and the mint vertical insertion caret. CRITICAL: fill the entire square background with uniform opaque deep teal #075E57. No transparency anywhere, no black background, no holes, no distressed edge noise. Redraw every contour as perfectly clean solid flat graphic shapes with smooth antialiasing. White arrow/text symbol #FFFDF5, mint caret #9FF1CD. Remove all outlines, all shadows, texture and gradients from symbol. Make arrowhead slightly smaller and more angular so it feels like a purposeful typography ligature rather than a puffy sticker. Keep the entire symbol centered within the central 62 percent width, with generous even padding. Single square full-bleed finished icon, no outer rounded tile, no mockup, no caption.
