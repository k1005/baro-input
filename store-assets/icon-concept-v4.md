## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset: one Android app icon for 바로입력, a utility that uses the camera to recognize printed text and insert it into text fields.
User's explicit direction: visibly express BOTH camera and input.
Design a unified bespoke mark: a bold warm-white camera body silhouette with softly rounded corners and a small top camera hump, unmistakably a camera. Replace its normal circular lens entirely with a clearly recognizable horizontal text input field cut out in the deep teal background color. Inside that dark input field place a short warm-white horizontal text stroke followed by a bright mint vertical I-beam text insertion cursor, clearly separated, optically centered. The camera's lens replacement is the input field itself, not an extra floating icon. The I-beam must read as a typing cursor with short top and bottom terminals, not a pause bar. One simple integrated camera-plus-input symbol. No circular lens needed. Tiny camera flash detail is unnecessary; focus on the two concepts.
Visual style: expertly crafted flat app brand mark, bold clean geometry, softly squared corners, excellent optical balance, high contrast, mature friendly utility. Full-bleed uniform deep teal #075E57 background. Camera silhouette warm white #FFFDF5. Cursor mint #9FF1CD. Flat opaque colors, perfectly smooth crisp edges. Main camera symbol takes about 62 percent canvas width and about 46 percent height, centered with generous safe margins for circular Android masking. Input field is generously sized within camera body, strokes readable at small sizes.
Output a single square finished opaque icon artwork. No transparency, texture, grain, gradients, shadows, outlines, 3D, bevels, glossy effects, outer rounded tile corners, mockups, captions, app names, Hangul, Latin characters, scan brackets, arrows, documents, keyboard grids, badges or extra decorative elements. Make it visually obvious: camera + editable text input, not video recording, not a credit card, not a battery.
